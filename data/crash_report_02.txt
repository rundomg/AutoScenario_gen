Vehicle #1 (2011 Toyota Prius) was stopped in the eastbound lane at a T-intersection, attempting to turn right to travel south. Non-Motorist #1 (72-year-old female) was crossing the roadway in a northerly direction, in an unmarked crosswalk with curb cuts from the sidewalk. The speed limit was 40 km/h. At the time of the crash, it was daytime, and the weather was clear. A tall fence bordering private property on the southwest corner could have been a visual obstruction early in approach for V1 and the Non-Motorist. As V1 initiated the right turn, the front of the vehicle contacted pedestrian #1 on their left side, impacting on the right-front corner of V1. The pedestrian fell to the ground, onto their left side along the right side of V1. V1 continued turning and the right front tire rolled over the left ankle of the pedestrian. The pedestrian came to rest approximately at the point of impact and V1 continued forward and came to rest shortly beyond the pedestrian final rest.
